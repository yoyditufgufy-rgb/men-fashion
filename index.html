How to Make Grilled Shrimp & Avocado Quinoa Salad 🥗🍤

Looking for a fresh, vibrant, and nutrient-packed dish that tastes like summer in a bowl? ☀️ This Grilled Shrimp, Avocado & Quinoa Bowl drizzled with zest pesto vinaigrette is the ultimate answer! 🥑 Loaded with protein, healthy fats, and zesty citrus flavor, it brings healthy gourmet dining straight to your kitchen table. 🌿✨

🛒 Ingredients You’ll Need

For the Bowl Base & Toppings:

  • Large Shrimp: 1 lb, peeled and deveined 🍤
  • Cooked Quinoa: 1.5 cups (fluffed and cooled) 🌾
  • Fresh Arugula / Baby Greens: 2 cups 🥬
  • Cherry Tomatoes: 1 cup, halved (yellow, red, and dark varieties) 🍅
  • Ripe Avocado: 1 large, diced 🥑
  • Olive Oil, Salt, & Black Pepper: For searing 🧂

For the Zesty Pesto Vinaigrette:

  • Basil Pesto: 3 tbsp 🌿
  • Extra Virgin Olive Oil: 3 tbsp 🫒
  • Lemon Juice: 2 tbsp (freshly squeezed) 🍋
  • Garlic: 1 clove, minced 🧄
  • Salt & Pepper: To taste 🧂

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Tutorial

  1. Whisk the Pesto Dressing 🥣
    In a small glass jar or bowl, combine the basil pesto, extra virgin olive oil, fresh lemon juice, minced garlic, salt, and black pepper. Whisk or shake vigorously until well combined, then set aside.
  2. Prepare the Quinoa & Greens Base 🌾
    In a large serving bowl, layer the fluffy cooked quinoa alongside fresh arugula greens. Toss gently so the greens lightly mix with the warm grain foundation.
  3. Sear the Shrimp 🍤
    Pat the shrimp dry with paper towels and season lightly with salt, pepper, and olive oil. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the shrimp for 2 to 3 minutes per side until pink, opaque, and beautifully seared.
  4. Assemble the Salad Bowl 🥗
    Arrange the seared shrimp over the quinoa base. Add the colorful halved cherry tomatoes and freshly diced avocado chunks around the bowl.
  5. Drizzle & Serve 🎉
    Generously spoon the zesty pesto vinaigrette over the shrimp, tomatoes, and avocado. Serve immediately with a fork and extra dressing on the side for a vibrant, power-packed meal! 🍽️💚

How to Make Crispy Bang Bang Chicken 🍗✨

Craving something crispy, creamy, and packed with a sweet and spicy kick? 🤤 Featured as one of the most popular new recipes, this Crispy Bang Bang Chicken is guaranteed to become your next obsession! 💥 Tossed in a glossy, flavorful sauce and sprinkled with toasted sesame seeds and fresh scallions, it brings restaurant-quality dining straight to your kitchen. 😋👇

🛒 Ingredients You’ll Need

For the Crispy Chicken:

  • Chicken Breast: 1.5 lbs, cut into bite-sized pieces 🍗
  • Buttermilk: 1 cup (for maximum tenderness) 🥛
  • Cornstarch & All-Purpose Flour: ½ cup each (for that ultimate crunch) 🥣
  • Egg: 1 large 🥚
  • Seasonings: 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper 🧂
  • Oil: For deep frying 🍳

For the Signature Bang Bang Sauce:

  • Mayonnaise: ½ cup 🍦
  • Sweet Thai Chili Sauce: ¼ cup 🍯
  • Sriracha: 1–2 tbsp (adjust for your spice preference) 🌶️
  • Honey: 1 tbsp 🐝

For the Fresh Garnish:

  • Sesame Seeds: 1 tbsp, toasted ⚪
  • Green Onions: 2 stalk chopped finely 🧅

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Tutorial

  1. Marinate the Chicken 🥣
    Place the chicken pieces in a bowl, pour over the buttermilk, and season with salt and pepper. Cover and let it soak in the fridge for at least 30 minutes to make the meat juicy and tender.
  2. Whisk the Bang Bang Sauce 🥣
    In a medium bowl, combine the mayonnaise, sweet Thai chili sauce, Sriracha, and honey. Stir until smooth and creamy. Set aside in the fridge while you cook the chicken.
  3. Prep the Crispy Coating 🌾
    In a wide dish, mix the cornstarch, flour, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. In a separate bowl, beat the egg. Dip each marinated chicken piece into the egg mixture, then dredge thoroughly in the flour blend, pressing firmly for maximum crust.
  4. Fry to Golden Perfection 💥
    Heat vegetable oil in a deep skillet over medium-high heat (around 350°F / 175°C). Carefully add the chicken in batches and fry for 4 to 6 minutes until golden brown and crispy. Drain on a paper towel.
  5. Toss and Serve 🎉
    Transfer the hot chicken to a large bowl, pour over the Bang Bang sauce, and gently toss to coat every piece evenly. Plate up, top generously with toasted sesame seeds and chopped green onions, and serve immediately! 🍽️✨

How to Make Homemade Green Enchiladas with Salsa Verde 🇲🇽🌶️

Craving authentic Mexican flavor that is tangy, cheesy, and packed with zesty goodness? 🤤 This easy Green Enchilada recipe featuring a rich, homemade green enchilada sauce is a ultimate family favorite ready in just 45 minutes! 💚 Stuffed with juicy shredded chicken, smothered in salsa verde, and baked to melty perfection, this dish brings restaurant-quality flavor straight to your dinner table. 🥑✨

🛒 Ingredients You’ll Need

For the Homemade Green Enchilada Sauce (Salsa Verde):

  • Tomatillos: 1 lb, husked and washed 🍏
  • Poblano Pepper & Jalapeño: 1 poblano and 1 jalapeño (for mild, smoky heat) 🌶️
  • Onion & Garlic: ½ white onion and 3 cloves garlic 🧄
  • Vegetable or Chicken Broth: 1 cup 🥣
  • Fresh Cilantro: ½ cup 🌿
  • Lime Juice: 1 tbsp (freshly squeezed) 🍋
  • Salt & Cumin: 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp ground cumin 🧂

For the Enchiladas & Assembly:

  • Corn Tortillas: 8 to 10 warm tortillas 🫓
  • Shredded Chicken: 2.5 cups cooked, shredded chicken 🍗
  • Monterey Jack / Oaxaca Cheese: 2 cups, shredded 🧀
  • Crumbling Cheese: Cotija or queso fresco 🧀
  • Garnish: Fresh cilantro and lime wedges 🍋🌿

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Tutorial

  1. Roast the Veggies 🥮
    Broil the tomatillos, poblano pepper, jalapeño, onion, and garlic on a baking sheet for 8 to 10 minutes until lightly charred.
  2. Blend the Green Enchilada Sauce 🌪️
    Transfer the roasted vegetables into a blender with cilantro, lime juice, cumin, salt, and broth. Blend until smooth, then simmer in a saucepan for 10 minutes to deepen the flavor.
  3. Fill and Roll the Tortillas 🫓
    Warm the corn tortillas so they become pliable. Mix shredded chicken with ½ cup of the green sauce and ½ cup of cheese. Place filling down the center of each tortilla, roll tightly, and arrange seam-side down in a baking dish.
  4. Sauce, Cheese, and Bake 🔥
    Pour the remaining green enchilada sauce generously over the rolled tortillas. Top with plenty of shredded Monterey Jack cheese and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 20 minutes until bubbly and golden! 🧀✨
  5. Garnish and Serve 🎉
    Sprinkle with crumbled queso fresco Cotija cheese and fresh chopped cilantro. Serve warm with fresh lime wedges for an irresistible feast! 🍽️💚

How to Make Fresh & Zesty Baja Fish Tacos 🌮🐟

Bring Taco Tuesday to life with these irresistible, vibrant Baja Fish Tacos! 🇲🇽✨ Packed with perfectly seasoned, flaky white fish, creamy avocado slices, tangy red cabbage slaw, and a drizzle of spicy hot sauce, every bite is an explosion of fresh coastal flavors. 🥑🌶️ Served on warm, charred tortillas, this healthy yet satisfying dish is super easy to assemble right at home! 🍽️😋

🛒 Ingredients You’ll Need

For the Seasoned Fish:

  • White Fish Fillets: 1.5 lbs (Cod, Halibut, or Tilapia), cut into bite-sized chunks 🐟
  • Olive Oil: 2 tbsp 🫒
  • Spices: 1 tsp chili powder, ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt, and black pepper 🧂

For the Cabbage Slaw & Creamy Sauce:

  • Red Cabbage: 2 cups, finely shredded 🥗
  • Lime Juice: 2 tbsp (freshly squeezed) 🍋
  • Sour Cream or Mexican Crema: ½ cup 🥛
  • Sriracha or Hot Sauce: 2 tbsp (for that extra kick!) 🌶️

For Taco Assembly & Garnish:

  • Small Tortillas: 6 to 8 warm flour or corn tortillas 🫓
  • Avocado: 1 large, sliced thin 🥑
  • Fresh Cilantro: ½ cup, chopped 🌿
  • Jalapeños: 1 pepper, sliced thinly 🌶️
  • Lime Wedges: For serving 🍋

👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Tutorial

  1. Prep the Tangy Red Cabbage Slaw 🥗
    In a medium bowl, toss the finely shredded red cabbage with fresh lime juice and a pinch of salt. Set aside so the cabbage softens slightly while absorbing that bright citrus zest.
  2. Season & Pan-Sear the Fish 🐟
    Pat the fish fillets dry with paper towels. Rub with olive oil, then season generously with chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat and sear the fish for 3 to 4 minutes per side until flaky and lightly browned.
  3. Warm the Tortillas 🫓
    Warm your tortillas directly over an open flame or in a dry skillet for 30 seconds per side until soft with subtle char marks.
  4. Assemble Your Tacos 🌮
    Spread a spoonful of crema on each warm tortilla. Layer with crunch red cabbage slaw, tender flaky fish pieces, and fresh avocado slices.
  5. Garnish & Serve Fresh 🎉
    Top generously with chopped cilantro, sliced jalapeños, and a drizzle of hot sauce. Serve immediately on a platter with fresh lime wedges for squeezing! 🍋🌮✨
